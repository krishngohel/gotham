Both nights are **short of the 60 minute target** by this model. The added missions come to 20
(n6) and 20 (n7) model minutes, 3 to 8 each, not the 8 to 10 the brief asked for. The honest
reading: each night roughly doubled, and neither reaches an hour. Timed by hand in the browser:
the level 2 escapes took 32 s (Night Six, into the park) and 36 s (Night Seven, onto a roof) going straight to cover (model 60 s); a first try at level 3 in the Loop could not be shaken from a roof and was lowered to 2. The pier rescue took 39 s teleporting
between its points (model 18 s + the walk).

## Firefly, played from the Batmobile by keyboard

Method: W, A, D and Shift pressed by a script with a 0.3 s reaction delay, steering by what a
driver can see (his position, the ring). It is a scripted driver, not a person; it wedges on
scenery, and what it shows is the geometry of the swoop, not how the fight feels.

Before the fix (270 s): 4 rams from 25 swoops, never out of the first phase (the armour reaching
zero restarts the phase and wipes the rams). On 13 swoops of 25 the ring was further from him than
the 44 m he flew low, so a car on the ring could not be touched by him. Eight more passes went
through the car within 1.3 m while it was slower than 8 m/s and counted for nothing, in silence.
The ring is a point around the car (`n7/firefly-before-ring-only.jpg`); nothing showed his line.

Fix (`src/bosses/defs/firefly.js`, `SWOOP`, `swoopLow`): he stays low until he is past the ring
(capped at 4.5 s), three discs mark his line on the ground between him and the ring, and a pass
through a car that is too slow says TOO SLOW with a line from him. The tell, the rams per phase,
the 8 m/s rule and the armour did not change.

After the fix (300 s, the same driver made to keep to the drive): the first phase fell in 31 s and
three rams landed in the first 36 s (`n7/firefly-after-2-ram.jpg`); then the scripted driver wedged
against the east barrier and he flew through the stationary car fourteen times. The line discs are
not in any of the screenshots taken (the camera was facing the other way at each tell), so their
look is unverified.

Verdict: a ram line was achievable before only when he happened to start within 44 m, and was not
readable. It is now always achievable from the ring. Whether it reads well in motion, and whether a
contact below 8 m/s should count, need a person at the wheel.
