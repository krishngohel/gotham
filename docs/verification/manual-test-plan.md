# Manual test plan (about 30 minutes, highest risk first)

Automated checks cannot cover these. Raw logs of what was automated are in `raw-merged/`.

1. **Two real PCs online, one pursuit.** Host a room, join by code. Get a wanted level: the guest should see cruisers, officers, the helicopter and roadblocks, get caught, and escape the cell.
2. **Two real gamepads in split screen.** Menus, hold View for the map and press A for a waypoint, Batcave stations. Known: the minimap overlaps the centre card and the radio straddles the split.
3. **A real old save.** Continue, open the Batcave Batcomputer, close it. The game must resume.
4. **New game to the Ace Chemicals step.** No police before Gordon's line; a chase is possible after it. Abilities and gadgets should arrive when the story teaches them.
5. **Accessibility.** Text size 1.5 with reduced motion and flash reduction: check lightning, the siren screen edge and boss distortion.
6. **Weather.** Drive in rain or snow (less traction) and glide in a storm (gusts).
7. **Audio by ear.** Music transitions, sound effects, radio, barks.
8. **One boss by hand on Normal.** Difficulty and length feel.

Not covered anywhere: Firefox, and a real integrated-graphics laptop on the Potato setting.
